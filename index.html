Bio

My name is Phat Lam. I graduated from Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City (VNU-HCM).

In January 2027, I will join the MS program in Computer Science at the University of New Brunswick (UNB), Canada, where I will work with Prof. Hung Cao at the Analytics Everywhere Lab (AELab).

My research interests include signal processing, machine learning for time series, multimodal learning, speech and audio analysis, and physiological computing. I am especially interested in practical models that remain efficient, interpretable, and useful in real-world sensing environments.
